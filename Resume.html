These are the Things I Enjoy

A little look into the things I enjoy, the places I want to explore, and the experiences I hope to have someday.

📝

Planning & Organizing

I usually spend part of my day planning and organizing my thoughts so I can manage my time better. I like making plans, arranging my schedule, and keeping things organized. I also genuinely enjoy cleaning because it helps me feel more comfortable and at peace.

🏓

Pickleball

I also love playing pickleball. It is a fun way for me to stay active, spend time with other people, and simply enjoy my free time.

✈️

Travel

I love traveling and discovering new places. For my international trips, I have been to South Korea in 2020 and Taiwan in 2025. For my local trips, I have visited Cebu, General Santos, Dumaguete, and Dipolog. Every trip gives me new experiences and memories that I get to carry with me.

🎧

Music & Concerts

I also love listening to music, especially when I am relaxing, traveling, or simply spending time by myself. I enjoy going to concerts because I love experiencing live music and creating memories while enjoying performances from artists I like.

🌊

Things I Want to Try

There are also many things I still want to experience in the future. I would love to learn how to freedive someday and experience being underwater. I also want to try skydiving because I think it would be an exciting and unforgettable experience.

Still learning, exploring, and trying new things.
